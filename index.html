BUYER CONFIDENCE

Clear answers before
you place an order.

Are exporters independently verified?

Registered exporter accounts undergo operator review before their listings can appear. Business document checks are recorded separately. Product safety, capacity and certifications require independent validation before contracting.

Can I pay through PakExportMatch?

Not currently. Quote acceptance creates a tracking record, not a payment or escrow transaction. Buyer and seller must agree payment and shipping terms independently.

Are the listed international buyers ready to purchase?

No. Our trade intelligence entries are research leads, official business contacts, customs databases and directories. They are not purchase orders, subscribers or guaranteed opportunities.

How can I request quotes from Pakistan?

Create a buyer account, submit a sourcing brief with specifications and quantity, and compare responses from eligible registered exporters. Supplier participation depends on who has been onboarded.
